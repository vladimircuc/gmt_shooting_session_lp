How every session runs

Strength and confidence behind every shot

Each lesson builds your shot from the ground up: exercises in the gym that carry over to shooting and puck handling, then technique work in the shooting lanes. Every drill is progressed or regressed to fit you.

  1. 01

    Gym stations

    Station work with exercises that correlate directly to shooting and puck handling, so you build the strength your shot actually uses.

  2. 02

    Shooting lanes

    Shooting technique on synthetic ice in our lanes, turning that strength into a harder, more accurate, more confident shot.

  3. 03

    Open 15

    After every session, stick around for 15 minutes to use the equipment and work on what you learned on your own.

    Included with every session

Who it's for

Every shooter. Every level.

From your first wrist shot to fine-tuning a college-level release, sessions meet you where you are. Coaches scale every drill up or down to fit each shooter's needs and goals.

  • BeginnersLearn the fundamentals and get comfortable getting pucks off the ice.
  • Experienced playersAdd strength, power and accuracy to the shot you already have.
  • Kids and adultsYouth players and adults all train here, each at the right level.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need to be a good shooter already?

No. Sessions are built for every skill level, from beginner to expert. Every drill can be progressed or regressed so it fits your needs and goals.

Is this for kids or adults?

Both. Kids, teens and adults are all welcome, and coaches adjust each drill to the shooter in front of them.

What happens in a session?

45 minutes of station work: exercises in the gym that correlate to shooting and puck handling, plus shooting technique in the shooting lanes. Afterward you can stay for 15 minutes to use the equipment and practice what you learned.

When are sessions and how many people are in each?

Saturdays at 8, 9 and 10 AM, year-round. Each session is capped at 12 shooters. If we're ever unavailable on a Saturday, we'll let you know ahead of time.

How much does it cost?

$40 per session. You'll see the total when you book.
